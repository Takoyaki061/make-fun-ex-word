Entry for the [One Word From Your Ex](https://make.fun/bounty/u-brave-eagle-12-one-word-from-your-ex-cfd97ba2) bounty on make.fun.

`main.py` writes exactly one word to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Nice

You brace for the insult and get a compliment that lands worse. "Nice" is the word said flatly, mid-shrug, over a drink: nothing wrong with the person, nothing remembered about them either. The indictment is not cruelty — it is that "nice" is all that survived the audit. One syllable, no explanation needed, and nearly everyone has dated someone they could accuse of nothing and describe only as "nice".

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Stdlib only, deterministic.
